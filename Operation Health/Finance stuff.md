### 1. Crucial Clarifications

- **US Shares & CHESS:** CHESS sponsorship **only applies to Australian shares** listed on the ASX (like Commbank, VGS, and DHHF). US shares (Apple, NVIDIA) are _never_ CHESS sponsored; they are always held under a custodian model globally via the US depository system.
    
    ID Advice
    
- **Vanguard Personal Investor:** This platform is **not CHESS sponsored**. It uses a custodian model for everything, including Australian ETFs. If CHESS sponsorship is a strict requirement for your ASX holdings, you should skip Vanguard Direct and buy their ETFs through a CHESS-sponsored broker instead.
    
- **CommSec vs. CommSec Pocket:** They are different. CommSec is the full CHESS-sponsored broker. CommSec Pocket is a micro-investing app that only lets you choose from 10 pre-selected ETFs. You cannot buy individual shares (like Commbank) or VGS/DHHF on Pocket.

### 3. Capital Gains Tax (CGT) Changes in 2027

You are spot on about the tax changes. The recent 2026-27 Federal Budget announced a massive overhaul to Capital Gains Tax.

- **What is changing:** Starting **1 July 2027**, the 50% CGT discount for assets held over 12 months is being abolished for shares. It is being replaced by an inflation-based cost base indexation method, alongside a **minimum 30% tax** on real capital gains.
    
- **Transitional Rules:** If you buy shares now, the current 50% discount will still apply to any capital gains you accrue _before_ 1 July 2027. For gains accruing _after_ that date, the new indexation and minimum tax rules will apply.
    

### 4. Other Important Concerns

- **The W-8BEN Form:** When buying US shares, the US government taxes dividends at 30%. You must fill out a W-8BEN form to declare your status as an Australian resident, which reduces this withholding tax to 15%. Fortunately, Stake, CMC, and CommSec all digitize and automate this form during sign-up.
