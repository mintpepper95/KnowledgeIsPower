This plan is designed to hit your **3kg weight loss goal** while simultaneously fixing the mechanical issue in your knee (Runner's Knee) so you can eventually run again pain-free.

### The Core Strategy

- **Weight Loss:** Achieved 90% through Diet (The Sandwich + Big Dinner).
    
- **Knee Fix:** Achieved by stopping impact (Running) and starting strengthening (Gym).
    
- **Calorie Burn:** Swapping Running for **Incline Walking** (burns almost the same calories, zero knee impact).
    

---

### Part 1: The Nutrition Plan (Daily Target: ~1,550 Calories)

**Schedule:** 16:8 Intermittent Fasting.

- **Skip Breakfast:** Black coffee/tea/water only.
    
- **First Meal:** 12:00 PM or 1:00 PM.
    
- **Second Meal:** 7:30 PM.
    

#### Meal 1: The "Power" Sandwich (~600 cal)

- 2 Slices Whole Grain/Sourdough Bread (Better energy than white bread).
    
- 150g Cooked Chicken Breast (shredded or sliced).
    
- 1/2 **Fresh Avocado** (The fat keeps you full).
    
- Lettuce/Tomato/Onion (Volume).
    
- _Condiment:_ Mustard or a light layer of mayo (don't drown it).
    

#### Meal 2: The "Repair" Dinner (~900 cal)

You have a large calorie budget here. Use it to get **High Protein** (to fix the knee) and **High Volume** (to feel full).

- **Protein:** 250g Steak, Salmon, or Chicken Thighs.
    
- **Carb:** 1 cup Boiled Rice or medium Potato.
    
- **The Secret:** Half your plate must be Green Vegetables (Broccoli, Asparagus, Spinach). Fried in garlic/little oil is fine.
